\section{Conclusion}

The Secure IoT Gateway Simulation for Resource Sensing (SIGS-RS) successfully implements and verifies a resilient, high-throughput, and privacy-preserving middleware platform designed for municipal smart city sensor grids. By addressing the critical vulnerabilities of legacy industrial SCADA systems---namely, cleartext telemetry transmission, lack of edge rate limiting, persistent PII leakage, and cascading database exhaustion---SIGS-RS establishes a comprehensive defense-in-depth architecture.

\subsection{Summary of Implemented Outcomes}
Through eighteen structured development and auditing phases, the platform has achieved the following core milestones:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Asynchronous Decoupled Ingestion:} Network ingress over MQTT/TLS 1.2+ (port 8883) and HTTP REST is fully decoupled from sequential database operations via an in-memory bounded \texttt{IngestionQueue}, ensuring that incoming socket connections return immediate 202 \texttt{Accepted} acknowledgments without waiting for relational persistence.
    \item \textbf{Rigorous Anomaly and Physics Inspection:} Incoming telemetry is validated against both temporal frequency bounds (sliding-window log capping sensor rates at 50 packets per 10 seconds) and physical reality equations (asserting non-negative water flow, valid pH ranges, power factor $\le 1.0$, and legitimate AQI limits). Hostile or malformed packets are dropped at the edge and dispatched to immutable audit sinks.
    \item \textbf{Privacy by Design and Fail-Secure Sanitization:} All geographic coordinates and hardware identifiers are permanently purged from telemetry payloads prior to persistence. If an unparseable or malformed key is detected, the sanitizer fails closed, discarding the packet rather than permitting accidental PII leakage into relational storage.
    \item \textbf{Fault Isolation and Circuit Breaking:} A three-state persistence Circuit Breaker protects downstream databases by transitioning to \texttt{OPEN} upon detecting write latencies exceeding 2,000~ms, preventing thread starvation and cascading system crashes.
    \item \textbf{Regulatory Governance and Immutable Auditing:} Dynamic privacy guideline updates immediately enforce a simulation lockout (BR-3) until signed off by an authorized Compliance Auditor. Furthermore, PostgreSQL triggers guarantee that security audit records cannot be modified or deleted by unauthorized database users.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Empirical Verification Summary}
The reliability and performance of SIGS-RS have been demonstrated through an extensive test suite comprising 580 automated tests, with 579 passed and exactly 1 test intentionally skipped due to the absence of a live external MQTT broker. Empirical microbenchmarking across Stages 0, 1, and 2 established that combined middleware processing achieves a $p99$ latency under 0.50~ms per packet---far below the 15.0-millisecond upper bound mandated by \texttt{REQ-PF-2}.

\subsection{Final Compliance Status}
While local verification confirmed sub-millisecond middleware latency and perfect packet accounting conservation, honest academic evaluation dictates that production cluster targets (such as \texttt{REQ-PF-1} sustaining 50,000 pkts/s across 10,000 sensors) remain architectural targets requiring multi-node Kubernetes clusters with dedicated hardware resources (16 vCPUs and 32~GB RAM per SRS §3.2). SIGS-RS delivers a verified, robust algorithmic foundation ready for cloud-native deployment.
